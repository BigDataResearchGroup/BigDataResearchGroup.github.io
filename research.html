Medical Diagnosis

Our research in Medical Diagnosis focuses on developing advanced algorithms and models for analyzing multimodal electronic health records (EHRs) to improve clinical decision making. We aim to leverage artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to extract valuable insights from complex medical data.

Key Research Areas

    Clinical risk prediction using machine learning and deep learning techniques

    The Dual-Focus Dynamic Multiple Imputation Approach For MNAR Missing Values In Medical Data

    Goal: In this paper, we propose a new method for imputing missing values in medical datasets.

    Methodology: this paper proposes the Dual-Focus Dynamic Multiple Imputation (DDMI) model for MNAR missing values in medical data. The DDMI uses piece-wise approximation to decompose complex relationships and directly calculates the impact of unobserved variables on patient indicators, avoiding distribution assumptions. In addition, the DDMI captures both population and individual-level information to predict missing values and then refines the results through multiple iterations, and the original information is combined in each iteration to mitigate information loss and improve convergence.

    Publication: Y. Zhai, Y. Li, H. Li and W. Luo, "The Dual-Focus Dynamic Multiple Imputation Approach For MNAR Missing Values In Medical Data," 2025 IEEE Congress on Evolutionary Computation (CEC), Hangzhou, China, 2025, pp. 1-8, doi: 10.1109/CEC65147.2025.11043054.

    Deep Learning-Based Clinical Risk Stratification for Chronic Diseases

    Goal: Developing a novel deep learning framework to stratify clinical risks for patients with chronic diseases using longitudinal health records.

    Methodology: This research introduces a transformer-based architecture that captures temporal dependencies in EHR data and integrates multi-modal clinical features to predict disease progression and adverse events.

    Publication: L. Zhang, H. Wang, W. Luo, "Transformer-based Clinical Risk Stratification for Chronic Diseases," Journal of Medical Informatics, 2024, in press.

Medical Treatment

Our Medical Treatment research focuses on developing AI-driven recommendation systems to optimize therapeutic interventions. We combine traditional medical knowledge with modern data science to create personalized treatment plans that improve patient outcomes.

Key Research Areas

  • Personalized chinese medicine recommendation systems
  • Acupuncture point selection algorithms
  • Automated control of personalized multiple anesthetics

AI in Stomatology

Our research in AI applications for stomatology focuses on developing computer vision and machine learning techniques to improve dental care, diagnosis, and treatment planning. We aim to enhance precision in dental procedures and improve patient outcomes through intelligent systems.

Key Research Areas

  • Orthodontic treatment planning and prediction
  • Dental restoration design and optimization
  • Oral cancer early detection and risk assessment
  • 3D dental image analysis and segmentation

Sentiment Analysis

Our sentiment analysis research focuses on developing advanced natural language processing and machine learning techniques to understand and predict human emotional states from various data sources. We apply these insights to mental health support and emotional well-being.

Key Research Areas

  • User sentiment analysis system
  • Music-assisted therapy for depression

Spatial-Temporal Data Mining

Our research in spatial-temporal data mining focuses on developing algorithms to extract knowledge and patterns from data that has both spatial and temporal components. This research has applications in urban computing.

Key Research Areas

  • Traffic flow prediction
  • Urban mobility pattern analysis
  • Spatial-temporal anomaly detection
